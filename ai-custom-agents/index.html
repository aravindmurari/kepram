What it can be

Built for the job

If it involves answering questions, moving a conversation forward, or working from your knowledge, we can build an agent for it.

Sales assistant

Qualifies leads, answers product and pricing questions, and moves buyers toward a booking or a handoff to your team.

  • Listen, educate, recommend, close
  • Lead capture and booking

Support & Q&A bot

  • Answers from your knowledge base
  • Escalates when it should

The engine

Same three parts,
every time.

Every agent is built from the same core engine. What changes is what we point it at.

01 — Goals

What it's for

We define exactly what the agent does and what a win looks like, so it stays pointed at your outcome instead of wandering.

02 — Knowledge

What it knows

We load your products, policies, and process as its knowledge base. Not a scrape of your website, the real thing.

03 — Guardrails

What it won't do

We set the rules for what it says, when to escalate to a human, and where to stop. Confident inside the lines, never past them.
